## TrendLens Smoke-Test Subset Scope

- **Sample Size:** 45 authentic vehicles from the training partition.
- **Body Types Covered:** Sedan (8), SUV (8), Coupe (8), Truck (7), Convertible (7), Wagon/Minivan (7).
- **Integrity Notice:** No images, vehicle specifications, or labels have been fabricated or synthesized. All images originate from the Stanford Cars benchmark.
- **Temporal Analysis Notice:** Model year values indicate vehicle manufacture/model release year, NOT sales volume or popularity over time.
